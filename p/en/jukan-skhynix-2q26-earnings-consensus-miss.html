SK Hynix's operating profit jumped 557% in a year, so why did it still miss consensus?

Open in the Stacks app → Read the original ↗

SK Hynix's Q2 operating profit came in at ₩60.5426 trillion, nearly ₩4 trillion short of the ₩64 trillion the market expected. Revenue missed as well, at ₩79.3187 trillion against a ₩84 trillion consensus. Just before the release, Meritz Securities had pencilled in ₩60.1 trillion for operating profit. That was ₩4 trillion below the market average, and it landed within ₩400 billion of the real figure. It also put pretax profit at ₩101.8 trillion, which would have been the largest quarterly profit a Kospi-listed company had ever booked. The miss looks less like a company turning bad than like expectations drifting up.

So why the consensus miss

That doesn't add up on the surface. Why would the company selling the most AI memory fall short of expectations. The answer runs the other way. SK Hynix carries an unusually heavy mix of expensive memory built for AI data centres, chips like HBM. This quarter, though, the prices that jumped hardest belonged to ordinary DRAM and NAND. Rivals that sell more of those chips took a larger share of the increase.

Same miss, different conclusions depending on which cycle you use as the baseline.

Why net profit is larger than revenue

Net profit was ₩93.9226 trillion. That is above the ₩60.5426 trillion operating profit, and above the ₩79.3187 trillion of revenue too. The company kept more money than it brought in from selling anything. Chips did not create that gap. The ₩3.9 trillion SK Hynix put into Toshiba Memory, now Kioxia, alongside Bain Capital in 2018 rose in value after Kioxia listed, and Meritz estimates the stake was cashed out during the second quarter. The direction matches the estimate from a day earlier. What is different is that these are now the company's confirmed numbers.

What do the 10 customer deals change

SK Hynix said it began mass-production shipments of HBM4 this quarter, with output set to expand in the second half. It also completed long-term supply agreements with roughly 10 major customers, deals that fix volume and price years ahead. The reason cited is demand for AI agents that decide on their own and carry out complex work. As AI moves past chatbots, more customers want years of supporting memory locked down in advance.

So what settles it

The question comes down to one thing. Which one previews next quarter: the ₩93 trillion net profit, or the operating miss. If operating profit is the true scorecard for the core chip business, watch whether the consensus miss repeats next quarter. If this miss came down to a single quarter in which only ordinary memory prices spiked, that gap should narrow. The Kioxia money is nearly all in. From next quarter, the operating profit line alone will show the chip cycle, with nothing else mixed in.

In three lines
  • SK Hynix posted Q2 revenue of ₩79.3187 trillion and operating profit of ₩60.5426 trillion, up 257% and 557% from a year earlier.
  • Net profit came in at ₩93.9226 trillion, more than the company's own revenue. An estimated ₩40 trillion-plus of that came from selling a Kioxia stake bought eight years ago.
  • Yet both lines missed consensus by about 5%. The market had been looking for ₩84 trillion in revenue and ₩64 trillion in operating profit.
