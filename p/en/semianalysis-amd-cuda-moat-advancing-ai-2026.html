0% TO 'GREAT CHANCE'
SemiAnalysis · 2026-07-25 · original: EN

SemiAnalysis, long tough on AMD, now says 'great chance.' Can it really break the CUDA moat?

Open in the Stacks app → Read the original ↗

SemiAnalysis has raised AMD's odds of catching Nvidia from a flat 0% to what it now calls a great chance. It is a paid research shop that digs deep on semiconductors, and it has been especially hard on AMD's software. That such a shop changed its mind is the heart of this piece. But it is worth reading why the same piece says it is still early.

What changed its mind?

Two things drove the upgrade: the chip and the customers. AMD's new MI455X accelerator is the first datacenter chip built on TSMC's 2-nanometer process. It stacks HBM4 memory 12 high for 432 gigabytes per package, more than the 8 stacks and 288 gigabytes Nvidia and Google use.

Nvidia & Google288GB, 8 stacks
FP8 compute20 petaflops

On memory capacity and process node, AMD genuinely leads. The spec edge is real. But the two risks SemiAnalysis flags are about production and software, not specs, so these numbers alone don't settle it.

The customers showed up too. Anthropic committed to 2 gigawatts of AMD chips, and AMD answered with an equity investment of up to 5 billion dollars in Anthropic. Microsoft, which had dropped AMD after quality problems, reversed course and will bring the new racks into its cloud. The end user is expected to be OpenAI.

So why only a 'chance'?

The headline and the body don't line up. That gap is the point of this card.

What the headline says

From 0% to a great chance of success. AMD is finally challenging Nvidia's software moat.

VS
What the same body says

Its key automated-test parity is still at 0%, and the 90% target was missed and pushed to October.

SemiAnalysis flags two risks. One is rack production. Early Helios units hit reliability problems in the backplane, so the ramp is slow. The other is heavier. AMD is chronically short of the in-house GPUs it needs to build and automatically test its software. SemiAnalysis writes that it has a single-digit multiple fewer than Nvidia. With too little test hardware, the pass rate that shows whether the software is verified to Nvidia's level lags behind.

Don't good specs settle it?

The same shop's own measurements from months ago point the other way. In a February 2026 inference benchmark, SemiAnalysis said AMD's earlier chip was completely beaten by Nvidia's B200 on low-precision (FP4) disaggregated inference. On open-source distributed inference, it said AMD trailed by more than six months. A spec-sheet lead and measured performance are still two different stories.

How is this different from the last card?

If the Jukan card views the same CUDA-moat debate from the DeepSeek and Huawei side, this one views it from AMD's side. Both say the software wall is getting lower. What is honest about this piece is that it stakes its conclusion on execution, not specs. Adding memory and lining up customers is done. What is left is verifying the software that runs on top to Nvidia's level, and that won't be settled until after October.

In three lines
  • SemiAnalysis, long tough on AMD's software, raised its odds against Nvidia from 0% to a great chance.
  • The basis is specs and customers. The new MI455X pairs a 2nm process with 432GB of HBM4, and Anthropic and Microsoft have signed on.
  • Yet the same piece flags two risks. The rack ramp is slow and automated-test parity is still at 0%. This is decided by execution, not specs.

Why it matters · It matters that an analysis shop known for being tough on AMD's software has changed its tune, but the two risks it flags, production ramp and internal test infrastructure, are execution problems rather than chip specs, so the real test is still ahead.

Worth asking · SemiAnalysis, long skeptical of AMD's software, now calls its odds against Nvidia's CUDA moat a great chance, even as it flags production ramp and internal testing risks. Do you think AMD can actually close the software gap this cycle, or will execution problems trip it up again?
