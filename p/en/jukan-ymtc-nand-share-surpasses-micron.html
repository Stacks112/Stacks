YMTC didn't overtake Micron in NAND share. It tied it, and that might be the bigger story

Open in the Stacks app → Read the original ↗

Chinese memory maker YMTC has overtaken both Micron and SanDisk in NAND flash market share, according to unnamed third-party data cited by semiconductor analyst Jukan, closing the gap with fourth-ranked Kioxia to just 0.4 percentage points. That specific claim, however, doesn't line up with what named research firms are actually reporting.

What named research firms show instead

TrendForce's official Q1 2026 NAND report doesn't rank YMTC among the top five suppliers at all, showing Samsung at 31.6%, SK hynix at 17.6%, and Kioxia, Micron, and SanDisk in a three-way tie at 13.9% each. Counterpoint Research data from around late June 2026 comes closer to Jukan's framing but still describes a tie rather than an overtake, putting Samsung at 29%, SK hynix at 14%, Kioxia at 14%, and YMTC, Micron, and SanDisk together at roughly 13%.

Combined Revenue of Top Five Global NAND Flash Suppliers Rose 83.7% QoQ for 1Q26 (TrendForce, 2026-05-25)

The growth rate is the real headline

What's well-documented is how fast YMTC got there. Counterpoint puts YMTC's NAND share at just 8% a year earlier, meaning its jump to roughly 13% represents around 445% year-over-year revenue growth, the fastest of any major NAND supplier. YMTC is racing to build two to three new fabs in Wuhan targeting roughly 500,000 wafers a month, using an increasingly domestic equipment supply chain, over 50% by some estimates, to work around US restrictions on advanced lithography and deposition tools, with a stated goal of 15% global NAND share by the end of 2026.

China's YMTC Global NAND Market Share Jumps to 13%, Now Tied With SanDisk (ZeroHedge, citing Counterpoint Research, 2026-06)
출처: zerohedge.com

Where the pressure actually lands

Analyst commentary frames the competitive threat as concentrated in commodity NAND, where YMTC and its DRAM counterpart CXMT are catching up quickly and pressuring SanDisk specifically, while Micron's larger enterprise and HBM-adjacent product mix is seen as somewhat more insulated. Both Micron and SanDisk have leaned on tight NAND supply and AI-driven demand to defend pricing this year, and a Chinese competitor at parity narrows the room to raise prices further.

Reading the claim correctly

Jukan's own figures, the overtake and the 0.4-point gap to Kioxia, trace to unnamed third-party data rather than a named research firm, and should be read as his source's specific claim rather than an independently confirmed ranking. The verified baseline, a three-way tie built on the fastest growth rate in the industry, is arguably the more durable story regardless of whose count is most precise.

In three lines
  • Analyst Jukan cited unnamed third-party data claiming China's YMTC overtook Micron and SanDisk in NAND share, closing to within 0.4 points of Kioxia.
  • Named research firms tell a slightly different story: Counterpoint's most recent data has YMTC, Micron, and SanDisk tied at roughly 13% each, up from just 8% for YMTC a year earlier, a jump of about 445% in revenue.
  • A three-way tie after growing from 8% in a year is still a major milestone for a company built under US export controls, even if it isn't quite the 'overtook two rivals' framing Jukan's source claims.
