The breakout Schiff describes is $1,200 below gold's January peak

Gold and silver appear to be breaking out just as Bitcoin is breaking down. That is not a coincidence. As investors seek exposure to a viable alternative to fiat currencies, some now seek an alternative to digital fiat, since it’s been losing purchasing power for over five years.

August 17, 2026

Gold and silver did rise this week, but both metals put in their all-time highs back in January. Gold traded at $4,383 an ounce on August 17, against a January 28-29 peak of $5,589.

Gold, silver rise as weak retail sales cut Fed-hike odds (Kitco, 2026-08-14)
출처: kitco.com

Peter Schiff called that move a breakout on August 17 and said bitcoin's decline alongside it was no coincidence.

January held the records

All three sit below their own highs. The peaks in gold and silver are seven months old, and silver has lost nearly half its value since. To call this a breakout you have to say what was broken through, and what was broken through is a two-month high.

Retrieved 2026-08-17 · Gold, Kitco spot on August 17 · Silver on August 14 · Bitcoin on August 17

Gold and silver each set a record within three days of the end of January and have been below those marks ever since. Silver has given back close to half of its price and is down 8.9% this year, while gold is up 1.7%. Bitcoin is down 29.6% over the same stretch. In a market where all three trade under their highs, two of them falling less is not the same evidence as money crossing from one to the other.

This is the weight of gold one bitcoin buys. Measured in dollars it is up 42% over the same span, but measured in gold, five years ago was the heavier coin.

Retrieved 2026-08-17 · Bitcoin divided by spot gold at the August 17 close of each year

Schiff wrote that bitcoin has been losing purchasing power for over five years. Measured in dollars that does not hold. Bitcoin was $44,695 in August 2021 and now trades 42% above that, and it cleared $120,000 last October. Measured in gold the story changes. One bitcoin bought 25.0 ounces in 2021, rose to 35.2 ounces by August last year, and buys 14.5 ounces now. It is indeed 42% lighter than five years ago, but that loss did not accumulate slowly across five years. It is packed into the ten months since last October's high. Inside one sentence the direction is right and the period is wrong.

Why silver swings harder

Silver draws on industrial and investment demand at once, so it moves further than gold on the same news. The global silver market is heading into a sixth consecutive year of deficit, with this year's shortfall estimated at 46.3 million ounces. Of the silver sitting in London vaults, the part not locked inside exchange-traded funds and therefore actually available to trade fell to roughly 136 million ounces at the end of September 2025, a record low. That is less than a third of one day's average volume. It is why the cost of borrowing the metal spiked to an annualized 39% at one point in October 2025.

The odds August 14 retail sales moved

Schiff's reading

Money that left paper currency is now leaving digital paper too, and going into gold and silver.

VS
Kitco's stated reason

July retail sales fell 0.6%, cutting September hike odds from 34% to 29%.

Looking at the same rally, one side sees trust in money and the other sees the odds of a rate hike. The money that actually moved fits neither cleanly. US spot bitcoin funds lost $390 million in the week of August 10-14, the largest weekly outflow in six weeks. The week before that they took in $853.5 million.

Bitcoin ETFs record largest outflow in six weeks as token stagnates (Bloomberg, 2026-08-17)
출처: business-standard.com

Gold funds took in $3 billion in July, ending two straight months of withdrawals. But June saw $8.9 billion leave, and the World Gold Council attributed that to rising real yields and a stronger dollar. August flows for gold funds have not been tallied yet. September decides it. If gold climbs back toward $5,589 even as the Fed raises rates, the bid really is buying protection from money itself; if it only rises when a hike is postponed, this move is a real-yield story. The two readings call for different behavior. In the first, gold is insurance that ignores the rate cycle; in the second, it sells off again the moment hike odds recover.

In three lines
  • Peter Schiff wrote that a breakout in gold and silver and a breakdown in bitcoin are happening in the same place.
  • On the surface that reads as money leaving paper for metal. Yet gold sits about 21% below its January 28-29 peak and silver 47% below its own.
  • What the metals printed this month is a two-month high, not a record, and the reason Kitco gave for the move was a weak July retail sales report.
