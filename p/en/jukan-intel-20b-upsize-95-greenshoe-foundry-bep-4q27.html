The 22.6 billion dollars Intel raised is ten more quarters of foundry losses

☄ Intel (INTC, Buy): Equity Offering Signals Confidence, Foundry and EMIB Continue to Advance

☀ Reiterate Buy and $136 target price: We view this equity offering as sending a positive signal. The CEO’s direct subscription…

August 14, 2026

Intel actually raised 22.6 billion dollars in this offering. It lifted the deal from an initial 15 billion to 20 billion, and the overallotment was exercised in full, which is how the final figure got there. 242.1 million shares went out at 95 dollars each.

Intel Announces Upsize and Pricing of $20 Billion Common Stock Offering (Intel Newsroom, 2026-08-10)
출처: newsroom.intel.com

The GF Securities report relayed by Jukan reads the deal as a confidence signal: institutional orders above 100 billion dollars, and chief executive Lip-Bu Tan subscribing at the offer price alongside a family member. Tom's Hardware reported that roughly a third of investors who put in orders received no shares at all.

The hole the money fills

Raised in this offering22.6 billion dollars
Foundry Q2 operating loss2.089 billion dollars
×10.8

A business losing about two billion dollars a quarter just took in ten quarters' worth of those losses at once. The breakeven date the company has named is six quarters away.

Intel Q2 2026 earnings release · retrieved 2026-08-16

Intel Foundry manufactures other companies' designs. For now Intel itself is effectively its largest customer, so revenue and losses grow together. The first-quarter operating loss was 2.437 billion dollars, and the 2025 full-year figure was 10.318 billion. Second-quarter revenue of 5.765 billion was up 31% year on year, and the loss did not narrow by anything like that much. This was not a company scraping for cash. At the end of the second quarter Intel held 29.7 billion dollars in cash and short-term investments against 50.5 billion in total debt. The raise flips it from a net debt position into net cash. That reads less like a forced sale from an empty vault and more like pre-funding several years of capital spending.

The first three lines already happened. The last two are still promises, and that is exactly where this card splits.

Intel Newsroom and SEC filings · retrieved 2026-08-16

A 4.8% larger share count thins existing holders by the same amount. That is why GF Securities kept its 136 dollar target while lifting its 2026 and 2027 earnings-per-share estimates by only 3% and 1%. The offer price sat more than seven dollars below the 102.50 close on 14 August.

What is confirmed and what is not

The strongest sentences in the GF report are the ones that cannot be checked from outside. The report puts second-quarter 18A yields at around 80%. What Intel itself said on its results call was qualitative: output rose meaningfully and yields are tracking ahead of expectations. The 80% figure does not appear in company material. Apple's 14A high volume production is the same kind of claim. Independent reporting goes only as far as a Taiwanese trade paper saying Apple is evaluating an improved 18A node, and Intel's second-quarter call does not mention Apple at all.

Unimicron says Intel's EMIB-T will not reach mass production until 2027 (DigiTimes, 2026-07-30)
출처: digitimes.com

On packaging the timing gap is sharper. EMIB joins two chips with a small silicon bridge instead of one large interposer, and EMIB-T adds a structure that carries power up from underneath. Unimicron, which supplies the substrate, said on 30 July that EMIB-T mass production will not start until 2027. GF raised its back-end revenue estimates on the strength of AWS adopting Trainium3 in 2027 and Google volumes expanding in the second half of 2027. The picture of rising revenue is not refuted, but the year that revenue starts is the same year the foundry is supposed to break even.

The 12 million dollars Lip-Bu Tan put in

The chief executive and a family member bought 12 million dollars of stock at the offer price. It is in the prospectus supplement, so the fact itself checks out. Against what the company raised, it is under 0.1%. You can read it as management putting its own money on the table; as funding, it rounds to nothing. Owning Intel today therefore means betting on a single line in the foundry segment's quarterly income statement. If foundry operating income clears zero in the fourth quarter of 2027, this raise was the money that laid the last span of the bridge. If it is still in the red then, ten quarters of cash bought time rather than a change of direction.

In three lines
  • Intel closed its share sale larger than planned, with the overallotment exercised in full and the share count up 4.8%.
  • On the surface it is a deal that drew far more demand than it needed: orders above 100 billion dollars, with a third of applicants getting nothing.
  • The question is the size of the hole the money fills. The foundry lost more than two billion dollars again last quarter, and the breakeven the company has promised is six quarters out.
