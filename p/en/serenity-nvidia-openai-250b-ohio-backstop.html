Nvidia Reportedly to Guarantee $250 Billion So OpenAI Can Build a $500 Billion Ohio Data Center

Open in the Stacks app → Read the original ↗

Nvidia is in talks to guarantee $250 billion of the financing for OpenAI's Ohio data center, the Wall Street Journal reported on July 26. The project's total cost could reach $500 billion, the largest single data center project announced so far. Serenity, an X account tracking chip and AI markets, summarized the report.

What's actually being built in Ohio

SoftBank's energy unit, SB Energy, is building a 10-gigawatt data center campus in Piketon, in southern Ohio. The site is a former uranium enrichment facility. That's roughly the output of five or six nuclear reactors combined. OpenAI would sign a long-term lease on the facility, and Nvidia would guarantee $250 billion of the construction financing.

Why a guarantee carries this much weight

A backstop means that if OpenAI can't repay, Nvidia steps in instead. For banks and bondholders, lending against OpenAI alone is a different bet than lending with the world's largest chipmaker standing behind it. OpenAI still doesn't hold an investment-grade credit rating. It's projected to lose about $14 billion this year on roughly $25 billion in revenue, with internal forecasts putting cumulative losses near $115 billion through 2029. That profile makes it hard to raise long-term debt directly for a $500 billion campus. Nvidia's guarantee is the workaround.

Where the circular financing complaint comes from

Serenity flagged the same report with a caveat. OpenAI is separately discussing a deal to buy up to $350 billion worth of Nvidia chips. Put together, Nvidia would guarantee $250 billion of OpenAI's debt while trying to sell $350 billion of its own chips into the data center that debt builds.

Investor Michael Burry reacted with a short line: "Around and around we go. Nvidia to guarantee $200 billion of ChatGPT's spending on $NVDA chips." He had just added to his Nvidia short position before posting it.

The skeptical read

Nvidia is financing demand for its own products, which makes the revenue growth look weaker than it seems, and leaves Nvidia holding the guarantee if OpenAI stumbles

VS
The bullish read

AI data center demand is large and certain enough that this guarantee is a manageable risk against Nvidia's own cash generation

Tech commentator Ed Zitron went further, calling it "insane on so many levels" and "about as bearish as it gets," and said the fact that SoftBank is building this should be scrutinized on its own. Both readings of the same numbers come down to one thing. How much of OpenAI's credit anyone actually trusts.

What decides this

The question comes down to one thing. Does a structure where Nvidia guarantees the debt that buys its own chips count as proof of demand, or a warning sign. If this deal closes as described and OpenAI still has no independent investment-grade rating, the circular-financing read holds up. If OpenAI secures its own rating out of this round, or the talks collapse, this report ends up as noise from an early negotiation. Right now neither company has confirmed anything, and both sides call it a discussion. What is clear is that the world's largest chipmaker is standing behind the debt of a company that has never turned a profit.

In three lines
  • Nvidia is reportedly in talks to guarantee $250 billion of financing for OpenAI's Ohio data center project.
  • On the surface, it looks like two AI infrastructure giants doubling down together.
  • But OpenAI still has no investment-grade rating, and it's separately discussing buying $350 billion of Nvidia chips with money the guarantee unlocks, which is why circular-financing critics are pushing back.
