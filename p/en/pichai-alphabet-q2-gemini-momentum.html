Pichai's own numbers: Gemini added 200 million users in five months, and Alphabet's cloud growth hit 82%

Open in the Stacks app → Read the original ↗

Alphabet CEO Sundar Pichai posted his own take right after the company's second-quarter 2026 results, calling it an amazing quarter where the company's AI investments are redefining what's possible across every part of the business, and the underlying numbers check out against Alphabet's actual earnings release.

The headline figures, confirmed

Alphabet's overall revenue reached $119.8 billion, up 24% year over year, and Google Cloud revenue hit $24.77 billion, up 82%, both matching Alphabet's own Q2 2026 report.

Alphabet Announces Second Quarter 2026 Results (Alphabet, 2026-07-22) ↗

Gemini's growth, in Pichai's own words

The Gemini app's monthly active users reached 950 million, up 200 million in five months from the 750 million figure reported back in February. The APIs powering Gemini and Alphabet's other AI models are processing 22 billion tokens per minute, up from a prior-quarter figure Pichai cited as more than 16 billion, the basic unit AI models use to process text. He also said Gemini Enterprise, the company's AI product for businesses, is now used by nearly 90% of the Fortune 100.

Spending with numbers to back it up

The results came alongside Alphabet's decision to raise its 2026 capex guidance to $195 billion to $205 billion.

Alphabet Raises 2026 Capex Guidance to $195-205B, Cloud Revenue Surges 82% (MLQ News, 2026-07) ↗

Pichai said he saw exciting momentum everywhere from Search to YouTube to the Gemini app, thanking partners and employees before hopping on the earnings call.

Users are up. Is the payoff up too

Gemini adding 200 million monthly users in five months and rising API traffic is an impressive growth story, and Pichai putting his own numbers behind it gives investors something concrete to check. But how much of that growth converts into revenue and profit for Alphabet, rather than free users padding a usage number, is still the open question.

In three lines
  • Alphabet's Q2 2026 revenue grew 24% year over year and Google Cloud growth accelerated to 82%, per the actual earnings release.
  • Pichai says Gemini's monthly users hit 950 million, up from 750 million five months earlier, with Gemini Enterprise reaching nearly 90% of the Fortune 100.
  • Alphabet also raised its 2026 capex guidance to $195-205 billion, putting real numbers behind the AI-spending story.

What happened next

Awaiting gradingCheck Alphabet's next quarterly report, expected around late October 2026, for an updated Gemini monthly active user count and API token throughput, to see whether growth keeps pace with this quarter's jump.
