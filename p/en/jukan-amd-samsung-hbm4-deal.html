AMD's 'Almost There' on the Samsung HBM4 Deal Checks Out. The 'Sat Through the Full Keynote' Detail Doesn't

Open in the Stacks app → Read the original ↗

AMD is nearing a definitive agreement with Samsung Electronics for large-scale supply of HBM4, the newest generation of high-bandwidth memory that sits next to AI chips. At AMD's Advancing AI 2026 event in San Francisco (July 22-23), CEO Lisa Su was asked about progress since the two companies' March HBM4 supply MOU and answered, "We're almost there."

AMD Turns to Samsung for HBM4 Memory as Lisa Su Races to Match NVIDIA's Rubin (wccftech, 2026-07-24)
출처: wccftech.com

Who showed up is also confirmed

Samsung's foundry business president Han Jin-man and Cho Sang-yeon, EVP of its US semiconductor operations (DSA), attended the event for final-stage contract talks, per a separate outlet.

AMD Launches 'Helios' with Samsung HBM4 (BigGo Finance, 2026-07-24)
출처: finance.biggo.com

But the 'sat through the two-hour keynote' detail isn't confirmed

Whether these two executives actually watched Su's full keynote, or how long it ran, appears in no article found. The Korea Herald's coverage says a separate, Samsung-hosted technical session at the event was run by more junior staff. Separately, Samsung chairman Lee Jae-yong attended the Sun Valley Conference in early July, a different trip that shouldn't be conflated with this one.

Confirmed

Han Jin-man and Cho Sang-yeon attended the event for final-stage talks

VS
Unconfirmed detail

That they sat through the full two-hour keynote

Helios's production ramp adds urgency

Su also said AMD's Helios AI server racks are now in full production, shipping by the end of Q3, raising the stakes on locking in HBM4 on time.

Samsung and AMD Expand Strategic Collaboration on Next-Generation AI Memory Solutions (AMD Newsroom, 2026-03-18) ↗

So how much of this should you believe

The core claim, a deal being close, is confirmed twice over: by Su's own words and by which executives showed up. What's layered on top, colorful specifics like watching the entire keynote, is a detail circulating without independent confirmation, and it's worth telling the two apart. Whether Samsung lands a major customer beyond Nvidia will be settled by the actual signed deal, not by how vivid the buildup sounds.

In three lines
  • AMD CEO Lisa Su really did say a Samsung HBM4 supply deal is 'almost there,' confirmed independently by two outlets.
  • Two Samsung executives, including its foundry division head, did attend for final-stage talks.
  • But the detail that they 'sat through the entire two-hour keynote' isn't confirmed anywhere.

What happened next

Awaiting gradingWhether AMD and Samsung actually sign the definitive HBM4 supply agreement around Helios's end-of-Q3 shipping start will be checked in mid-October 2026.
