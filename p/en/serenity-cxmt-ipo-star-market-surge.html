China's CXMT Surges 470% on Debut, Becoming a $489 Billion Chipmaker Overnight

Open in the Stacks app → Read the original ↗

China's DRAM maker ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT) surged 472% on its trading debut on Shanghai's STAR Market on July 27. Its market cap jumped from $85.5 billion to $489 billion in a single day. Market watchers, including the X account Serenity, reacted with disbelief to what was Asia's biggest IPO of the year.

How unusual was this listing

CXMT priced its IPO at 8.66 yuan and closed its first day at 49.50 yuan. It raised up to 66.6 billion yuan, beating the previous STAR Market record set by chipmaker SMIC in 2020 (53.2 billion yuan). Reports say the surge pushed CXMT's market cap past Intel's, making it China's largest listed company by that measure.

Why did money pile in this hard

Demand for AI server DRAM is running well ahead of supply, and that's lifted every memory maker's valuation. According to chip analyst Jukan, Nomura initiated coverage on listing day with a price target of 116 yuan and a 20x P/E multiple, twice Micron's valuation. If that target holds, CXMT's stock would be 13.4 times its IPO price and its market cap would reach 7.76 trillion yuan. Jukan's own reaction: "This is insane."
