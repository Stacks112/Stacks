Glossary

market cap

It is what the market says it would cost to buy the whole company. Set beside a profit or cash flow forecast, it shows how much of that future the market actually believes. When forecast profits sum to more than the market cap, the market is not taking the forecast at face value, and where that gap comes from is where an investment judgment starts.
