JPMORGANJPM.US

JPMorgan Chase is the largest bank in the United States and one of the most systemically important financial institutions in the world, spanning consumer banking, investment banking, trading, and asset management. It was formed in 2000 from the merger of J.P. Morgan & Co. and Chase Manhattan, and has been led since 2005 by Jamie Dimon, who is widely regarded as the era's most influential banker. The Bank for International Settlements ranks it as the single most complex bank globally, which makes the eventual handover of its leadership one of the most closely watched questions in finance.
