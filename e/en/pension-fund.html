Glossary

pension fund

Institutions like Korea's National Pension Service that invest decades ahead with money collected for future pension payments. Managing hundreds of billions of dollars, a single shift in their asset allocation moves market supply and demand. They defend allocation ratios rather than chase moves, which often puts them on the mechanical-selling side (rebalancing) after sharp rallies.
