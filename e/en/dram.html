Glossary

DRAM

Think of it as the desk where a computer or phone lays out whatever it's working on right now. Unlike NAND, which is for storage, DRAM loses its contents when the power goes off. Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron split most of the global market, so DRAM pricing is the benchmark for reading their earnings and the memory cycle.
