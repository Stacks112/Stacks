Glossary

Bank of Japan

Rates are decided by a vote of the nine-member Monetary Policy Meeting, which includes the governor and two deputies and convenes eight times a year. It is the central bank that held rates near zero for longer than any other, so when it raises them the arithmetic of money borrowed cheaply in yen and invested abroad changes as well. Currency intervention itself is decided by the Ministry of Finance, with the bank acting as its agent in the market.
