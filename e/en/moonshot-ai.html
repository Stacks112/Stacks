MOONSHOT AI

A Chinese AI startup founded in Beijing in 2023 by Tsinghua University alumni; its formal name is Beijing Moonshot AI. It made its name with the Kimi chatbot and drew large investments from backers including Alibaba and Tencent. Its July 2026 open-weight model Kimi K3 coined the phrase 'Kimi moment,' casting it as a leading challenger trying to reach frontier performance on a fraction of the budget.
