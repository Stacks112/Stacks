Semiconductors

SK HYNIX000660.KS

Global memory-chip maker, renamed SK hynix in 2012 after joining SK Group. Its DRAM and NAND business leads the HBM (high-bandwidth memory) market that feeds Nvidia's AI accelerators, making it a prime beneficiary of the AI supercycle. Headquartered in Icheon, Korea, with fabs in Icheon, Cheongju and Wuxi, China.

CEO Kwak Noh-jung · Founded 1949 · Listed 1996.12 · HQ Icheon, South Korea · Exchange KOSPI · Website skhynix.com
