depreciation

It matters most where capital spending is heavy. A data centre built this year keeps producing depreciation charges for years afterwards, so profit can fall while cash flow holds up, or the reverse. When a company's capital spending jumps, its depreciation line tells you when that spending will start eating reported profit.
