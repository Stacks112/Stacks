Section 338

It has almost never been invoked, so the case law around it is unusually thin. It drew attention again after the Supreme Court struck down emergency-powers tariffs in February 2026 and the administration reached for it as an alternative route. A different statute means a different challenge process and a different timetable for companies that want to fight the duty.
