Glossary

CW

CW lasers supply a constant beam that downstream modulators encode with data, as opposed to pulsed lasers used in applications like some lidar. Suppliers who make CW lasers, such as Sivers Semiconductors, can sell into both the lidar and optical-networking supply chains.
