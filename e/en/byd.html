Electric Vehicles

BYD1211.HK

Shenzhen based BYD started in 1995 as a battery maker before moving into electric vehicles, and has grown into the world's largest EV maker by volume, selling both battery electric and plug in hybrid models under its own brands as well as premium lines like Denza and Yangwang. It is vertically integrated, making its own batteries, chips and semiconductors in house, which has helped it undercut rivals on cost. BYD has expanded aggressively into export markets, especially Europe, Latin America and Southeast Asia, as Chinese authorities pull back domestic subsidies. It trades in Hong Kong and Shenzhen and is chaired by founder Wang Chuanfu.
