Glossary

DUV

Short for deep ultraviolet. A single exposure can print down to roughly 28nm-class circuits, and multi-patterning, overlapping exposures on the same spot, stretches that to about 7nm-class. With EUV machines export-blocked, how far China can push DUV defines the limit of its chip self-sufficiency, which is why news of domestically built DUV tools moves markets.
