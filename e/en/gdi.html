GDI

Gross Domestic Income (GDI) totals all income generated in an economy, wages, corporate profits and investment income, and should in theory equal GDP since one side's spending is another side's income. Splitting GDI into wage share versus profit share shows who is capturing the gains from economic growth, which is why investors watch the wage share as a signal for corporate margin durability and inequality trends.
