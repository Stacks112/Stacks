POET TECHNOLOGIESPOET

It builds optical engines for data centres on an interposer technology that joins electrical and optical signalling on one platform. Revenue is still measured in hundreds of thousands of dollars a quarter, and the company's plan is oriented toward capacity of one million units a month by the end of 2027. The money for that build-out has come from successive share issues rather than from product sales.

CEO Suresh Venkatesan · HQ Toronto, Ontario, Canada · Exchange NASDAQ · Website poet-technologies.com
