Memory Chips (NAND Flash)

KIOXIA285A.T

Spun off from Toshiba's struggling memory business in 2018 and renamed Kioxia in 2019, the company is one of the world's largest makers of NAND flash memory and SSDs, tracing its lineage to the team that invented flash memory in the 1980s. It listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange in December 2024 after years as one of the most-watched IPO candidates in Japan. Riding the AI-driven memory supercycle, it named a new president and CEO, Hiroo Ota, effective April 2026.
