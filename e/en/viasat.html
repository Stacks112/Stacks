Satellite Communications

VIASATVSAT.US

Founded in 1986 in Carlsbad, California, Viasat provides high-speed satellite broadband and secure communications for military and commercial customers. Beyond its core satellite business, the company holds a portfolio of technology patents, including flash-memory error-correction methods, which it has enforced through US courts against chipmakers.
