Glossary

balance sheet

When a central bank buys bonds or lends against them, the asset lands on one side and the newly created money on the other, so the sheet grows. Shrinking it pulls money back out, which is why the direction of a central bank's balance sheet is read as a policy stance in its own right, separate from the level of interest rates.
