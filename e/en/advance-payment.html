Glossary

advance payment

When supply is short, customers pay up front to hold production slots. The supplier books the cash as a liability, not revenue, and works it off as shipments go out. It also makes the contract hard to walk away from, so a wave of prepaid long-term deals signals that buyers see securing volume as urgent.
