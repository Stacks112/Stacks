Glossary

CPU

The chip that directs nearly everything a computer does, from running the operating system to executing programs. Its few powerful cores excel at complex sequential work, which is where it parts ways with the GPU's thousands of parallel calculations. Even in AI servers, marshaling GPUs and moving data remains CPU work, which is why Intel and AMD server CPU prices and volumes keep showing up in AI infrastructure stories.
