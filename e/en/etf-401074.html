leveraged ETF

The key word is daily: because leverage resets every day, a market that whipsaws back to where it started still leaves a leveraged ETF with accumulated losses, a effect called volatility drag. It's a tool for short-term directional bets, not long-term holding, which is why regulators put brakes like deposit requirements on retail investors who pile in.
