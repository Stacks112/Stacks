Glossary

HBM

High Bandwidth Memory: DRAM dies stacked vertically and bonded next to the GPU, essential for AI chips. Far pricier and higher-margin than commodity DRAM, it drives the memory supercycle, with SK hynix leading supply to Nvidia.
