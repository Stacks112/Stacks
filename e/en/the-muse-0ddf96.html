The Muse

Founded in Jeju in December 2020 with just 10 million won in capital by three Berklee College of Music alumni, The Muse raised a sub-1-billion-won Pre-Series A round in 2023 to develop girl group RESCENE, which debuted in March 2024. After early albums underperformed, a viral moment involving the group's leader in May 2026 let the company close its Series A early at 2 billion won rather than the 5 billion won it originally sought, betting on a higher valuation before its next round.
