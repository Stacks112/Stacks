Lidar / Photonics

AEVAAEVA

Aeva makes 4D lidar sensors for autonomous vehicles and industrial uses, built on frequency-modulated continuous-wave (FMCW) laser technology. In August 2026 it launched an Optical Connectivity division to apply that photonics expertise to AI data-center links, signing a joint development deal for deployment at an undisclosed hyperscaler. Its core lidar business remains small, with quarterly revenue around $6 million.

HQ Mountain View, California, USA · Exchange NASDAQ · Website aeva.com
