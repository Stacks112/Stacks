Glossary

corporate bond

Corporate bonds let companies raise debt directly from investors rather than through bank loans. Investment-grade bonds are issued by companies seen as low default risk and typically pay lower yields; when money flows out of investment-grade bond funds, it can signal investors want less exposure to corporate credit risk, even if stock markets look calm.
