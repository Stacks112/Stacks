Glossary

FCF

Operating cash flow minus capital expenditure, known as FCF. Unlike accounting profit, it's what actually lands in the bank, and dividends, buybacks and debt repayment are all funded from it. Record profits alongside negative free cash flow means the company is pouring more into facilities, AI data centers being the current case, than it earns.
