disclosure

Facts that can move a share price, from earnings to big contracts to asset sales, cannot be released selectively: they go out on a prescribed form, such as an 8-K or 10-Q in the US. A filing precedes and outranks the news story built on it, so when the numbers disagree the filing is the one to check.
