KOSPI

It sets the total market value on January 4, 1980 at 100 and tracks the multiple since. Cap-weighted giants like Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix dominate its moves, so the chip cycle often decides which way the KOSPI goes. It occupies the same place in Korea that the S&P 500 does in the US.
