Brent

Roughly two-thirds of the world's traded crude is priced off Brent, so it reacts first to anything affecting seaborne supply, including shipping chokepoints. WTI is the US equivalent, and the gap between the two shows how much of a move is a global shipping story rather than a domestic American one.
