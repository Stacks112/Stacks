design win

Once a chip design is fixed it is hard to switch suppliers midway, so the moment a part is designed in effectively reserves years of revenue. But it usually takes two to three years before that product is actually built and sold, so there is a long lag between announcing a design win and revenue rising. That lag is why opinions split on how to price the design wins of a company whose current results are poor.
