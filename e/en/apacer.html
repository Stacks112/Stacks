Memory modules

APACER8271

It does not fabricate chips. It buys them from Samsung, SK hynix and Micron and mounts them on boards. That places it behind hyperscalers and device makers in the allocation queue, so the volume it receives works as a thermometer for how tight the memory market is. Its own niche is industrial and embedded storage.

CEO C.K. Chang · Founded 1997 · HQ Taipei, Taiwan · Exchange TWSE · Website apacer.com
