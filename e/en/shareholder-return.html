Glossary

shareholder return

A dividend hands out cash directly, while a buyback has the company purchase its own shares and shrink the count outstanding. The amount returned divided by market cap is the shareholder return yield, and once a company states what share of free cash flow it will return, the yield can be computed from a cash flow forecast alone. That is also why a wrong cash flow forecast makes the yield wrong with it.
