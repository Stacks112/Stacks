Optical components

APPLIED OPTOELECTRONICSAAOI

An optoelectronics maker founded in Texas in 1997. It is vertically integrated, building everything from laser chips to finished optical transceivers in house. It supplies optical modules to data centers, telecom carriers and cable operators, and counts large cloud providers among its customers. Founder Thompson Lin has served as chief executive since the company started.
