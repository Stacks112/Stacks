EMIB-T

EMIB-T embeds through-silicon vias in a small silicon bridge to carry power and data directly between memory and logic chips, rather than bonding everything onto one large interposer the way CoWoS-L does. Intel claims the approach avoids some of the warping and yield problems that come with scaling a single giant interposer, and says it can support packages up to 12 times reticle size, more headroom than TSMC's current packaging offers, positioning Intel as a potential alternative supplier if TSMC's packaging hits a wall.
