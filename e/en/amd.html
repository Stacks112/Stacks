AMDAMD

Advanced Micro Devices designs CPUs, GPUs and AI accelerator chips, competing directly with Nvidia in AI data center hardware and with Intel in PC and server processors. Under CEO Lisa Su, the company has expanded from a distant challenger into a key supplier for hyperscale AI buildouts, with its MI-series accelerators and Instinct platform positioned as the main alternative to Nvidia's GPUs. AMD also supplies EPYC server CPUs used widely in cloud data centers.
