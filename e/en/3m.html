3MMMM

Founded in Minnesota in 1902, 3M is a diversified US industrial company spanning consumer products like Post-it Notes and Scotch Tape, industrial adhesives and abrasives, and specialty materials including PFAS-based compounds. It spun off its healthcare business as Solventum in 2024 to refocus on industrials and materials. Facing environmental regulation and litigation over PFAS, it had previously said it would wind down related production by the end of 2025.
