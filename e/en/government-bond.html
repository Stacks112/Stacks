Glossary

government bond

Governments issue bonds when taxes do not cover spending. Buyers get principal and interest unless the state defaults, so these are treated as the safest asset and their yield anchors every other rate, from mortgages to corporate debt. When a foreign central bank dumps them, the issuing country's long-term rates get pushed up.
