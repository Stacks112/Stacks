RUBIN

Nvidia's next AI accelerator generation after Blackwell, pairing a Vera CPU with a Rubin GPU in rack-scale systems (NVL72). Shipments are planned for Q3 2026, and its adoption of HBM4 memory has sharply raised memory's share of total cost versus the prior generation.
