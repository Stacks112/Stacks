SMIC688981 / 0981

Founded in 2000 and headquartered in Shanghai, SMIC is China's largest semiconductor foundry and a centerpiece of the country's push for chipmaking self-sufficiency. Cut off from the most advanced lithography tools by US export controls, it has focused on maximizing output from older equipment and adopting domestic alternatives as they become available, making it a bellwether for how fast China can close the gap with TSMC and Samsung.
