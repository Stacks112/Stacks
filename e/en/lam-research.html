LAM RESEARCHLRCX

Lam Research makes the etch and deposition equipment used to carve and build the microscopic structures inside memory and logic chips, with a particular strength in the tools needed for advanced 3D NAND and DRAM production. Its equipment is heavily used by memory makers like Samsung, SK hynix, and Micron as they push into more complex chip architectures such as 3D-stacked DRAM, making Lam a direct beneficiary of the memory industry's capacity race. Like its peers, longer lead times at Lam translate into slower capacity additions across the memory supply chain.

CEO Tim Archer · Founded 1980 · Listed True · HQ Fremont, California, US · Exchange NASDAQ · Website lamresearch.com
