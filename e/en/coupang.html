COUPANGCPNG

Coupang operates Korea's dominant e-commerce marketplace built around next-day and same-day 'Rocket Delivery,' backed by a nationwide network of fulfillment centers. It listed on the NYSE in 2021 and later expanded into Taiwan. Founder Bom Kim stepped back from the CEO role in December 2025 following scrutiny over a major customer data breach, with Harold Rogers taking over as chief executive.
