Central bank

New York Fed

Based in the same city as Wall Street, it runs the trades that turn the Federal Reserve's policy rate into a market rate. It also publishes the quarterly Household Debt and Credit Report, which is where most cited figures on US household borrowing and delinquency come from. Its consumer credit data is built on a sample of individual credit records from Equifax.

CEO John Williams · Founded 1914 · HQ New York, United States · Website newyorkfed.org
