Duration

Duration is roughly a bond's average payback period and, crucially, its price sensitivity to interest rates: the longer the duration, the more the price falls when yields rise. A bond with a 13-year duration loses about 9% of its value if yields climb 0.72 points, which is why long-dated bonds carry far more interest-rate risk than short ones.
