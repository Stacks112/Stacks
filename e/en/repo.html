Glossary

repo

Legally a sale, economically a secured loan: a bond is handed over for overnight or few-day cash and bought back at a set price. It is the main plumbing through which banks and central banks square up their daily cash, and a spike in repo rates reads as a warning that cash is drying up somewhere.
