Glossary

bitcoin

It runs without a central bank: network participants jointly verify a shared ledger. Its supply is capped in code, which earns it the nickname digital gold, though its swings dwarf gold's. Spot ETFs and corporate treasury holdings have wired it into the stock market, so it now moves with equities and rates more often than it used to.
