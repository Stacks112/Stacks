Glossary

spot price

In memory chips, most volume ships under contract prices set with big customers, while the spot price covers small, immediate purchases. Spot tends to move first and more violently, so a widening gap between a surging spot price and a lagging contract price is watched as an early signal that contract prices, which actually drive memory makers' revenue, may soon be revised upward.
