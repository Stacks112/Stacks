Glossary

base die

If HBM is a warehouse of stacked DRAM, the base die is the ground-floor logistics hub. Every bit the upper floors exchange passes through it, so as HBM generations advance, the base die's throughput and heat handling become the bottleneck. That's why building this layer on a foundry's most advanced logic process, rather than a memory process, has become the new front in the HBM race.
