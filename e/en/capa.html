Glossary

CAPA

Industry shorthand for capacity, usually counted in wafer starts per month for chipmakers. Adding capacity takes years, from construction to tool move-in, so today's expansion decisions become supply several years out. Capacity added in booms returning as oversupply in busts is the basic mechanics of the memory cycle.
