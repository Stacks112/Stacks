TCO

Total Cost of Ownership adds up everything it costs to own and run a piece of hardware over its useful life, the purchase price plus power, cooling, floor space, networking and maintenance. For AI chips, power and cooling costs can rival or exceed the sticker price over a few years, so a chip that costs more upfront but uses less electricity per unit of performance can still be the cheaper choice on a TCO basis, which is why chipmakers and cloud providers increasingly market performance per dollar of TCO rather than raw speed.
