Glossary

MLCC

MLCCs (multilayer ceramic capacitors) are passive components that stabilize power and filter noise in electronic circuits. A single AI server can use thousands, and higher-performance boards need more and better ones. Supply is dominated by a handful of Japanese, Korean and Taiwanese makers, so when AI demand surges, these humble parts can develop long lead times and become an overlooked constraint on hardware output.
