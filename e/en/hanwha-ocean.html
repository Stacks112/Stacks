Shipbuilding & Defense

HANWHA OCEAN042660.KS

Formerly Daewoo Shipbuilding & Marine Engineering (DSME), it became Hanwha Ocean in 2023 after Hanwha Group acquired it. One of Korea's three big shipyards, it builds LNG carriers, container ships, submarines and naval vessels. Since 2024 it has led Hanwha's push into the US, buying Philadelphia's Philly Shipyard together with Hanwha Systems, and it is positioning for US Navy construction and MRO work as Washington looks abroad to rebuild shipbuilding capacity.

CEO Kim Hee-cheol · Founded 1973 · HQ Geoje & Seoul, South Korea · Exchange KRX · Website hanwhaocean.com
