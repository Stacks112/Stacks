Optical Components

LUMENTUMLITE

Spun out of JDSU and listed in 2015. Its Cloud & Networking segment supplies optical chips and modules to data center and AI infrastructure customers, while Industrial Tech builds laser systems for semiconductor, display and EV manufacturing. Because bigger AI clusters need more optical links between GPUs, its demand tracks data center buildouts closely.

CEO Michael E. Hurlston · Founded 2015 · HQ San Jose, California, USA · Exchange NASDAQ · Website lumentum.com
