Glossary

LTA

A Long-Term Agreement locks in volumes and prices for years. With 'take-or-pay' terms, the customer pays even if it doesn't take delivery, effectively guaranteeing the supplier's future revenue. In a cyclical industry like memory, a wave of LTAs signals demand turning structural.
