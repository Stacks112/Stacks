utilization rate

A chip plant carries most of its costs, depreciation and staff, whether it runs full or half empty. So when utilization rises, the same fixed cost is spread over more units and margins improve faster than revenue does. When orders fall and lines idle, the plant loses money regardless of the price it charges, which is why utilization is checked before price when reading foundry results.
