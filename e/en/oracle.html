Enterprise software / cloud infrastructure

ORACLEORCL

Founded in 1977, Oracle built its business on database software before pushing hard into cloud infrastructure to compete with AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud. Under founder Larry Ellison, the company signed large AI-cloud contracts including with OpenAI, funding the buildout with a rapid increase in debt. In September 2025 Safra Catz moved from CEO to Executive Vice Chair, with Clay Magouyrk and Mike Sicilia taking over as co-CEOs.
