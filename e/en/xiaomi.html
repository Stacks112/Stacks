XIAOMI1810.HK

Founded in 2010 by Lei Jun, Xiaomi built its business on low-margin, high-volume smartphones and a broad ecosystem of connected home devices. It listed in Hong Kong in 2018 and has since expanded into electric vehicles. As one of the world's largest phone makers, its shipment guidance is closely watched as a read on memory-chip demand from the downstream device side.
