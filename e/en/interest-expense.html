Glossary

interest expense

Any borrower, corporate or sovereign, owes interest each year on top of the principal, and the amount is set by the size of the debt together with market rates. Bonds issued after rates rise carry a heavier interest expense, so the same amount of debt costs different sums depending on when it was borrowed.
