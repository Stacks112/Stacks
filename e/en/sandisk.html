Semiconductors

SANDISKSNDK.US

SanDisk was spun off from Western Digital in 2025 as a standalone NAND flash and storage company, reuniting the SanDisk brand with a pure-play memory business. It competes directly with Samsung, SK hynix, Kioxia, Micron, and China's YMTC in the cyclical NAND market, where pricing power depends heavily on supply discipline and AI-driven storage demand.

CEO David Goeckeler · Founded 1988 · HQ Milpitas, USA · Exchange NASDAQ · Website sandisk.com
