rare earth

Rare-earth elements like neodymium and dysprosium are refined into magnets that are small but essential in EV motors, wind turbines, robotics, and missile guidance systems. China controls over 90% of global rare-earth magnet production and has used export licensing as leverage in trade disputes, making the supply chain a recurring flashpoint between Washington and Beijing.
