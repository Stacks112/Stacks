Glossary

GPU

Originally built to draw game graphics. Its design, splitting an image into countless dots and computing them all at once, turned out to fit AI math, making it the core of the data center buildout. Nvidia effectively monopolizes the market, and when AI infrastructure stories say 'chips,' they usually mean GPUs.
