BLACKROCKBLK

Founded in 1988, BlackRock is the world's largest asset manager with trillions of dollars under management across index funds, ETFs and institutional mandates. Its iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT), launched in January 2024, quickly became the dominant spot Bitcoin ETF by assets, making BlackRock's flows a widely watched gauge of institutional crypto demand.
