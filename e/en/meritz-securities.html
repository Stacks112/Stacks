MERITZ SECURITIES

Founded in 1973 as Hanil Securities, it went through several name changes before becoming Meritz Securities in 2000. Meritz Financial Group took it fully private in 2022-2023 by delisting it as a wholly owned subsidiary. Its earnings estimates on Korean semiconductor and memory names are widely cited.
