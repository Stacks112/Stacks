Founded in 1987 by Morris Chang, the world's first and largest chip foundry. It effectively monopolizes leading-edge production for Apple, Nvidia and AMD, with ~90% share at advanced nodes. Its Taiwan concentration puts it at the center of geopolitical risk, hence new fabs in the US and Japan.
