Glossary

YIELD

Yield is the percentage of chips cut from a single wafer that pass quality tests and work as intended. A low yield means more defective chips are scrapped, raising the cost of producing each usable chip. Yield improvement is one of the main ways chipmakers cut costs and boost margins over a product's life.
