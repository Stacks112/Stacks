Analog semiconductors

MACOMMTSI

An analog chipmaker that began in 1950 as Microwave Associates. It builds radio frequency, millimeter wave, optical and networking products on gallium arsenide, gallium nitride and silicon photonics processes. Its main markets are aerospace and defense, telecom infrastructure, data centers and satellite communications. Stephen Daly has been chief executive since 2019.
