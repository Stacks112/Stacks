Glossary

collateral asset

Collateral lowers a lender's risk, so pledged loans carry lower rates. The best collateral is stable and easy to sell, which is why government bonds dominate. If its value falls the lender demands more, and failure to post it forces a sale. That is the channel through which one drop feeds the next in a stressed market.
