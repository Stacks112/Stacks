Founded in 1998, PayPal helped popularize paying online and later spun off from eBay in 2015 to become an independent public company. It acquired Braintree and Venmo in 2013 to fend off newer rivals like Stripe, but growth has slowed and the company has cycled through multiple CEOs since, making it a subject of takeover speculation.
