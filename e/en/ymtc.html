YMTC

Founded in 2016 as part of China's push for chip self-sufficiency, YMTC develops 3D NAND flash memory using its own Xtacking architecture. Cut off from the most advanced foreign lithography and deposition equipment by US export controls, it has still scaled domestic 3D NAND output with heavy state financial backing, and has climbed the global NAND market share rankings faster than most analysts expected.
