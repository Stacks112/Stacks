KOLON TISSUEGENE950160.KS

Korean biotech under the Kolon Group, founded in 1999, developing the cell and gene therapy TG-C (branded Invossa in Korea) for degenerative knee osteoarthritis. Won domestic approval in 2017, but sales were halted in 2019 after regulators found one of its components was mislabeled. The company has since run large US Phase 3 trials to try to revive the drug. Listed on KOSDAQ in 2017.

CEO Jeon Seung-ho · Founded 1999 · Listed 2017.11 · HQ Rockville, Maryland, US · Exchange KOSDAQ · Website tissuegene.com
