TREASURY YIELD

When investors sell Treasuries, prices fall and yields rise, and vice versa. Rising long-term yields raise the discount rate used to value future corporate profits, which hits high-growth and AI stocks the hardest, and also raises the government's own borrowing costs. Yields are watched closely as a real-time gauge of inflation expectations and risk appetite.
