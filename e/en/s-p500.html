S&P500

It holds the 500 largest listed US companies by market value and serves as the de facto answer to whether the US market rose or fell today. Because it's cap-weighted, giants like Apple, Nvidia and Microsoft dominate its moves, and there are stretches when 'the S&P 500 rose' means roughly the same thing as 'big tech rose.'
