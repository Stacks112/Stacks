Glossary

export control

A tariff makes something expensive so less is bought. An export control means it cannot be bought at all. Applied to an item with no substitute, the other side cannot obtain it at any price. Chip equipment and rare earths have been at the centre of this tool in recent years for the same reason: only a handful of countries can make them.
