implied correlation

Implied correlation is derived from options prices and estimates how tightly the components of an index, like the S&P 500, are expected to move as one. High readings appear in panics, when everything sells off together; very low readings signal 'dispersion', where individual stocks trade on their own fundamentals. For investors it is a lens on hidden concentration: a calm index with low correlation can be driven by a few mega-caps while the average masks big winners and losers underneath, rewarding stock-pickers and flattering passive benchmarks.
