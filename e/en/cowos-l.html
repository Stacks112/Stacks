CoWoS-L

CoWoS-L (Chip on Wafer on Substrate, Local silicon interconnect) is TSMC's packaging platform for building today's largest AI chips, including Nvidia's GPUs, by bonding several silicon dies and stacks of high bandwidth memory onto a large interposer. It is central to how big an AI chip package can get, so any limit on how far CoWoS-L can scale becomes a real ceiling on chipmakers' roadmaps, not just a TSMC production issue.
