Glossary

unrealised gain

Accounting rules require holdings such as stakes in other companies to be carried at their latest valuation, and the change flows through net income. That makes reported profit move with the price someone else set in the most recent funding round or market close, and it cuts both ways: a lower valuation writes the same amount back out.
