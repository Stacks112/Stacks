TrendForce is a Taiwan-based technology market intelligence firm best known for its DRAMeXchange memory-pricing data and supply chain research across semiconductors, displays and green energy. Its shipment and order-backlog surveys are widely cited by chipmakers, investors and industry press tracking memory and component cycles.
