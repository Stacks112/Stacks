SAMSUNG ELECTRO-MECHANICS009150.KS

Founded in 1973 as a Samsung Electronics affiliate, Samsung Electro-Mechanics makes multi-layer ceramic capacitors (MLCCs), camera modules and semiconductor substrates used across smartphones, cars and data center hardware. It is one of the world's largest MLCC makers alongside Japan's Murata and Taiyo Yuden, and has increasingly supplied components for AI servers.

CEO Jang Duk-hyun · Founded 1973 · HQ Suwon, South Korea · Exchange KOSPI · Website samsungsem.com
