Semiconductor test equipment

FORMFACTORFORM

It makes the equipment used in semiconductor test. Its main product is the probe card that lands needles on chips while they are still on the wafer, and it also sells test systems and metrology. Products that stack several chips, like HBM, and products that package optics next to the die, like co-packaged optics, add test steps, which puts the company where revenue moves early when those technologies spread.

CEO Mike Slessor · Founded 1993 · HQ Livermore, California, USA · Exchange NASDAQ · Website formfactor.com
