Glossary

LPDDR

Short for Low Power DDR, a DRAM standard designed to sip power in battery-driven devices. The trailing numbers and letters (LPDDR5X, LPDDR6) mark generations, each faster and more power-efficient than the last. As on-device AI grows, the spec race in this low-power memory matters more.
