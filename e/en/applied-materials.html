APPLIED MATERIALSAMAT

Applied Materials is the world's largest supplier of semiconductor manufacturing equipment by revenue, making the machines used for deposition, etching, and other steps in building chips on silicon wafers. Its tools are used across nearly every major chipmaker's fabs, from logic foundries like TSMC to memory makers like Samsung and SK hynix, giving it broad exposure to how much the whole industry is spending on new capacity. Because its order book reflects capex plans across the entire chip sector, Applied Materials' results are often read as a barometer for the health of the semiconductor cycle.
