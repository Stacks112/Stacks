The Vague 'Mega Deal' Preview Turned Into a Real $200B Broadcom Deal for Samsung and a $500B Nvidia Deal for SK Hynix

Open in the Stacks app → Read the original ↗
J
Jukan@jukan05
𝕏

Kim Yong-beom, the presidential chief of staff for policy, said Samsung and SK hynix are expected to announce “very large-scale” contracts with leading U.S. technology companies during President Lee Jae-myung’s visit to Silicon Valley.

July 24, 2026

Ahead of President Lee Jae-myung's Silicon Valley trip, presidential chief of staff for policy Kim Yong-beom previewed 'very large-scale' supply contracts for Samsung Electronics and SK hynix with major US tech firms, without naming which companies or whether the deals covered memory, foundry work, or both.

The vague preview turned into hard numbers within days

On July 25, what actually got announced was far more specific than the preview. Samsung signed a roughly $200 billion, five-year deal (through 2030) with Broadcom covering HBM supply for AI accelerators, sub-2nm foundry production and advanced packaging.

Samsung wins $200 billion order to supply chips to Broadcom (Fortune, 2026-07-25)
출처: fortune.com

SK hynix's side involved both Nvidia and Microsoft

SK Group signed a roughly $500 billion letter of intent with Nvidia covering multi-gigawatt AI data-center buildout and long-term HBM supply, and separately reportedly extended HBM4 supply to Microsoft. SK's combined big-tech partnerships were put at roughly $750 billion over five years.

NVIDIA Secures SK Hynix HBM Memory In $500B AI Infrastructure Deal (HotHardware, 2026-07-25)
출처: hothardware.com

Worth flagging: the roughly $250 billion figure attributed to Microsoft is derived by subtracting Nvidia's $500 billion from SK's $750 billion total in one syndicated report, not a standalone confirmed number.

The presidential office confirmed the total itself

At the San Francisco AI Summit, Kim Yong-beom said Korean firms and US big tech agreed to roughly $950 billion in semiconductor cooperation, pointing directly to the Samsung-Broadcom and SK hynix-Nvidia deals as the substance of his earlier preview. The Korea Herald report carrying those remarks describes the Samsung-Broadcom agreement as a $200 billion memorandum of understanding and folds SK's side into a single $750 billion figure covering Nvidia and other global tech firms, so the $500 billion Nvidia-only breakout above does not appear in that article.

Korea, US tech giants set sights on $950 billion in chip partnerships: Seoul (The Korea Herald, 2026-07-25)
출처: koreaherald.com

What this preview actually left behind

It's unusual for commercial contracts normally negotiated privately between companies to land on numbers that match a presidential-level preview this closely. The thing to watch now is whether Samsung keeps expanding beyond Nvidia and Broadcom, and whether the Microsoft figure gets an independent confirmation of its own.

In three lines
  • Korea's presidential office previewed 'very large-scale' deals for Samsung and SK hynix in Silicon Valley without naming partners.
  • What actually landed was a roughly $200 billion Broadcom deal for Samsung and a roughly $500 billion Nvidia deal for SK hynix.
  • It's a rare case where a vague government preview was confirmed by specific numbers within days.
